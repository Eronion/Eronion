## Brandon Willy Viglianisi

ML & AI engineer based in Modena, Italy. I build computer vision and LLM systems and
take them from research prototype to production — mostly in manufacturing, logistics
and healthcare.

Co-founder and lead ML engineer at **Ardora AI**, where we develop CertifAI Med, a SaaS
platform that uses LLM-powered agents to automate biomedical regulatory documentation.
Alongside that I work as an ML engineer at **GoatAI** on industrial vision systems, and
teach applied generative AI to developers and managers.

Most of my recent work lives in private and client repositories, so this profile is

readable — payslips, product passports.
